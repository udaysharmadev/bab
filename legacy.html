    Hold a key. Keep rowing.

    Choose a key, then press a letter or number. Arrow keys always work.

    Hold W/↑ forward · S/↓ reverse · A/← D/→ turn. Each player controls their own paddle.

    Hold to repeat complete strokes. Combine forward and turn to curve. Release to lift your paddle; the boat keeps its momentum. Each player controls their own paddle, so coordinate with your crew. Red-marked obstacles break the boat on contact.

    On touchscreens: hold a directional button. With a standard controller: left stick or D-pad rows and turns; Start opens the menu. Centre the stick after closing a menu before rowing.

    Escape opens the game menu.
