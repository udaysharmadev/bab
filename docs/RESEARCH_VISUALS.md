# Bad at Boating — water, crew and dangerous-world research

19 October 2026. Research input for the user's requested graphics, player modelling, water depth/shine/physics, obstacles and title-menu upgrade. These are source-backed implementation recommendations, not a claim that AAA production quality or the complete PRD has been achieved. The user's latest direction overrides proposals in attached design documents.

## What was actually reviewed

Read the official technical pages below, Guerrilla's developer interview, the existing gameplay research, PRD §§6, 8–11, and installed Three.js **0.186.1** `Water.js` and `RoomEnvironment.js`. No commercial game binary was played or instrumented. ## Concrete implementation priorities

1. **Water:** coherent mesh displacement; moving fine normals; sun glint; Fresnel/environment response; shallow-to-deep color; contact/shore foam; wake tied to hull speed and blade splash tied to actual immersed effort. Preserve a visible safe lane on low graphics. Avoid calling artistic depth color measured optical refraction.
2. **Hero boat:** stronger bow/stern shape, layered wooden strakes, weathered grain, selective wet sheen, rope, seams and metal fasteners. Existing collider remains a simplified physical proxy: decorative bolts must not decide a crash. Keep independently breakable panels legible through the shared wreck sequence.
3. **Four readable people:** rear-camera silhouettes, distinct headwear/hair and clothing, recognizable vest/boots, hands visibly gripping the shafts, shoulder/torso response to each server-derived blade pose. Color alone must not carry identity. Never animate an energetic catch while the authoritative blade is dry or disabled.
4. **Hazards:** authored pointed silhouettes for spikes; distinct warning rings/flow for a vortex; honest moving geometry if a timed obstacle is added. Art and collider use one shared obstacle definition. Lethal hull touch follows the user's current rule; harmless paddle bracing and near misses retain their existing contract.
5. **World and title:** warm directional light, cooler atmospheric distance, stronger banks and a foreshadowed waterworks landmark. A live cinematic title composition with compact Play with Friends / Join Crew / Practice choices should remain a game menu. No dashboard panels or marketing page are required.

These priorities match PRD §11's hero boat → four stroke animations → local water/contact effects → landmarks ordering. The proposed lava stretch is an optional later authored encounter: it needs a coherent route, advance warning and real failure geometry/field before being advertised as playable content. A warm emissive rock alone would not implement lava gameplay.

## Physical and performance boundaries

- Server remains authoritative at 60 Hz. One spatial water function supplies buoyancy and blade-relative current. A whirlpool needs bounded, smooth rotational/inward velocity and a coherent surface depression; test finite values at its center and continuity at its edge. It is a gameplay field, not a Navier–Stokes simulation.
- Cosmetic shading must use synchronized simulation time while connected, including pause, reset and failure. Purely decorative fine ripples may differ without changing forces.
- Moving hazards require server-owned deterministic timing and safe checkpoint reset semantics. A client animation clock cannot decide collision pose.
- Preserve distinct safe/unsafe silhouettes without bloom, shadows or particles. Share the authoritative failure identity among clients; visual fragments cannot invent another collision.
- Measure renderer draw calls, frame samples and shader errors on representative views. Headless browser timings are not proof of 1080p/60 on the as-yet undefined reference hardware. Retain a low-quality path and never change colliders or flow to improve graphics speed.
- Acceptance still includes actual four-human observations, readable warnings at 907×510, distinct crew silhouettes, contact accuracy, repeated resets/reconnects and longer GPU/memory profiling. Better screenshots alone do not close those PRD gates.
